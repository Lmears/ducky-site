DucKy

Free, pay what you want. DucKy is a multiband ducking effect with three ways to trigger it: Tempo: ducks at the Duck Rate, from 1/1 to 1/16, following your DAW's transport. MIDI: ducks on every MIDI note you send it. KerBy: ducks on every kick from KerBy, with no routing, even as you change Rate or turn up Glitch. Tempo and MIDI work without KerBy. You can pick a preset or draw your own duck. Depth sets how far it dips, and Smooth rounds it off. Split it into 1, 2 or 3 bands, each with its own Depth, Rate, Smooth and shape, so you duck only the bands you want, each by its own amount. Runs on macOS 11 or later (AU and VST3) and 64-bit Windows 10 or later (VST3). There's no AAX version, so it won't run in Pro Tools. Updates are free. Your download link always has the latest, and you'll get an email when one's out. Pairs with KerBy: it ducks to KerBy's kick with no routing. Following KerBy is tested in Ableton Live; in other DAWs, use MIDI if it can't find KerBy.
