DucKy, a ducking effect by Slypork

Duck anything, band by band.

A multiband ducking effect. It follows the beat, your MIDI notes, or every kick from KerBy, Slypork’s kick and bass instrument.

Free, pay what you want (US$5 suggested). AU and VST3 on macOS. VST3 on Windows.

Drawing controls

Drag a point to move it, or drag just off a line to move it. Click a line or double-click anywhere to add a point, and double-click a point to take it away. Hold Shift to land on the grid, and Alt and drag a line to bend it (Alt-double-click to straighten it). Ctrl+Z undoes, and with Shift redoes.

On the beat, the notes or the kick.

Follow the tempo

Ducks at the Duck Rate, from 1/1 to 1/16, following your DAW’s transport.

Follow the notes

Ducks on every MIDI note you send it.

Follow KerBy

Ducks on every kick from KerBy, with no routing, even as you change Rate or turn up Glitch.

1, 2 or 3 bands.

Each band has its own Depth, Rate, Smooth and shape, so you can duck just the lows, just the mids, or each by a different amount.

What you get

macOS 11 or later: AU and VST3, in an installer. 64-bit Windows 10 or later: VST3, in a zip. There’s no AAX version, so it won’t run in Pro Tools.

Updates are free. Your download link always has the latest, and you’ll get an email when one’s out.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. It’s pay what you want, and you can pay nothing.
Do I need KerBy?
No. Tempo and MIDI work without it.
Will it work in my DAW?
It’s an AU and VST3 effect. Following KerBy is tested in Ableton Live; in other DAWs, use MIDI if it can’t find KerBy. If yours does something odd, tell me: dev [at] slypork [dot] net.
